---
name: Relationship model
description: The product distinction between dog matching, messaging, and trusted-pack relationships.
---

Use one private matching funnel: an owner expresses interest in another dog, the recipient explicitly selects “Interested too” to create a mutual match, and matched owners can message. Keep trusted packs as a separate, later, explicit relationship for walking and pet-care trust. Never auto-convert historical pending requests into matches.

**Why:** The user chose this model to avoid mixing algorithmic recommendations, messaging, public packs, and trusted pet-care relationships. A casual playdate match should not automatically imply pet-sitting trust.

**How to apply:** Use dog-first language throughout discovery and match surfaces. Present pending interest privately, label reciprocal connections as matches, and reserve “pack” terminology for later trust and care-sharing features.
