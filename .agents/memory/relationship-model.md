---
name: Relationship model
description: The product distinction between dog matching, messaging, and trusted-pack relationships.
---

Use one private matching funnel: an owner expresses interest in another dog, the recipient explicitly selects “Interested too” to create a mutual match, and matched owners can message. A Local Park Pack is a public, location-based community for walks, playdates, and meetups. Keep private walking and pet-care trust as a separate, later, explicit Trusted Circle relationship. Never auto-convert historical pending requests into matches.

Signed-in members may start a Local Park Pack for an Austin park that is not already listed. Member-started packs are public, auto-join their creator, and must be labeled Community-created; only verified City records receive the official off-leash designation.

**Why:** The user chose this model to make the parks and public packs experience understandable without implying that joining a park community grants pet-sitting trust. A park membership or casual playdate match should never automatically imply private care access.

**How to apply:** Use dog-first language throughout discovery and match surfaces. Present pending interest privately, label reciprocal connections as matches, use “Local Park Pack” only for park communities, distinguish City-verified from Community-created packs, and use “Trusted Circle” for future care-sharing relationships.
