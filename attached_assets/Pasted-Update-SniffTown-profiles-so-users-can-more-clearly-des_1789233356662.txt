Update SniffTown profiles so users can more clearly describe:

1. What they are looking for
2. What their dog is like
3. What kinds of dogs their dog tends to do well with

The goal is to improve matching while keeping the profile setup simple and fast.

CORE MATCHING PRINCIPLE

Prioritize:

* Proximity
* Shared intent
* Dog compatibility

Do not make this feel like a long dating-app questionnaire.

---

SECTION 1: WHAT ARE YOU LOOKING FOR?

Allow users to select one or more:

* Dog walk
* Playdate
* Park meetup
* Dog-friendly date
* Friendship / meet new people
* Regular walking buddy
* Dog sitting swap / trade-off
* Occasional drop-off help
* Other

Allow multiple selections.

Optional short note:

“Anything else you're looking for?”

Example:
“Would love another dog owner nearby for evening walks and occasional weekend playdates.”

---

SECTION 2: ABOUT YOUR DOG

Use simple selectable traits.

Temperament:

* Friendly
* Playful
* Calm
* Energetic
* Shy
* Independent
* Gentle
* Protective
* Excitable
* Selective with dogs

Energy level:

* Low
* Medium
* High

Play style:

* Likes to chase
* Likes wrestling
* Gentle play
* Fetch
* Mostly likes walking together
* Prefers people to dogs
* Still learning social skills

Dog size:

* Small
* Medium
* Large
* Extra large

---

SECTION 3: DOG COMPATIBILITY

Add:

“My dog usually does well with…”

Selectable options:

* Small dogs
* Medium dogs
* Large dogs
* Puppies
* Adult dogs
* Senior dogs
* Calm dogs
* High-energy dogs
* Male dogs
* Female dogs
* Most dogs
* Depends on the dog

Also allow:

“My dog may NOT be a good fit with…”

Selectable options:

* Small dogs
* Large dogs
* Puppies
* High-energy dogs
* Male dogs
* Female dogs
* Dogs that play rough
* No major restrictions

Keep this optional.

---

SECTION 4: WALK / MEETUP PREFERENCES

Add optional preferences:

Typical availability:

* Morning
* Midday
* Afternoon
* Evening
* Weekdays
* Weekends

Preferred meetup type:

* Neighborhood walk
* Park walk
* Dog park
* Trail / hike
* Patio / dog-friendly social meetup

Preferred distance:
Use location/proximity rather than making this overly complicated.

---

SECTION 5: PET-SITTING / TRADE-OFF INTEREST

If user selects:

* Dog sitting swap
* Occasional drop-off help

Show an optional sub-section:

Comfortable with:

* Watching another dog for a few hours
* Half day
* Full day
* Overnight
* Trading dog-sitting favors
* Paid pet-sitting referrals

This should be optional and should NOT dominate the profile.

---

PROFILE DISPLAY

Do not dump every field onto the profile.

Show a clean summary such as:

Looking for:
Dog walks · Playdates · Friends

Frank:
Friendly · Playful · Medium energy

Usually does well with:
Medium/large dogs · Calm or playful dogs

Usually walks:
Evenings · Weekends

---

MATCHING / DISCOVERY

Use these fields to improve discovery.

Prioritize dogs/users who:

1. Are geographically nearby
2. Share at least one meetup intent
3. Have compatible dog preferences
4. Have overlapping availability

Do not create a complicated compatibility score yet.

Instead, surface simple explanations such as:

“Nearby + both looking for evening walks”

or

“Good fit for playdates”

or

“Both interested in dog-sitting swaps”

---

IMPORTANT UX PRINCIPLE

Keep onboarding simple.

Use:

* chips
* checkboxes
* multi-select buttons
* short optional text fields

Avoid:

* long forms
* required essays
* too many screens
* complicated scoring

Users should be able to complete the profile quickly and refine it later.

The goal is to help someone quickly answer:

“Would I actually want to meet this person and dog?”
